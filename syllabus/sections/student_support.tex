\section*{Inclusivity, Accessibility, Title IX, and Wellbeing}
\textbf{Inclusivity and Accessibility.} The instructional team strives to create an equitable and inclusive learning environment that ensures the full participation and success of all students in the course. We expect all class members to be respectful of diverse perspectives and individuals, and to use inclusive language in both written and oral work. We recognize that students learn in different ways, and we encourage students to communicate with the instructional team about individual learning needs and how this course can best support them.

Students with a documented disability, or who believe they may require accommodations, should contact Access and Neurodiverse Student Success (ANSS) as early as possible. Academic accommodations require official registration with ANSS. Once registered, students should promptly notify the instructor so that accommodations can be implemented in a timely manner. ANSS contact information: \href{mailto:dss@rpi.edu}{dss@rpi.edu}; 518-276-8197.

\textbf{Title IX.} Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute recognizes that to maintain a healthy, safe, and vibrant living and learning community, it must continue to foster an environment free from gender inequality and sexual violence. RPI's Title IX office administers Title IX policies and thoroughly investigates all Title IX complaints. \href{https://sexualviolence.rpi.edu/resources}{Title IX resources} are available for all members of the campus community.

\textbf{Student Wellbeing \& RPI Campus Resources.} All students' holistic wellbeing, including physical, mental, and emotional health, is of importance throughout the semester. Good health and wellbeing practices contribute to overall academic success. Feel free to communicate with your instructor throughout the semester about your wellbeing, especially if you are facing wellbeing or health challenges that may affect your attendance and performance in the course and/or need assistance getting in touch with campus academic support and wellbeing resources.
