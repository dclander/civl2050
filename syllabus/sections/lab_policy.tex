\section*{Laboratory Experiments}
Students will complete three laboratory experiments designed to develop proficiency with the experimental method: observation, modeling, prediction, and evaluation of fluid behavior. A theoretical model is used to predict experimental outcomes and is compared with measured results. A formal report for each experiment is required. \textbf{All three lab reports must be submitted to pass the course.}

Lab execution is scheduled by section; report due dates and pre-lab submission dates are listed separately because the writing and analysis burden is the main scheduling constraint.
