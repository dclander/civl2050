\section*{Course Organization and Assessment}
The course is structured around lecture, laboratory experiments, homework, attendance, and examinations. Lecture meetings are two 110-minute meetings per week. Laboratory experiments are conducted in small teams during the assigned lab section.

\subsection*{Attendance Policy}
Attendance is mandatory for this course. Because consistent presence is essential for learning and participation, attendance will count toward your final grade.
\begin{itemize}
\item Attendance will be recorded using the Blackboard Attendance tool.
\item You may miss up to three classes without penalty during the semester. These absences are intended to cover minor illnesses, travel, or other personal circumstances. No documentation is required for the first three absences.
\item For any additional absence, notify the instructor in advance when possible (e.g., for university travel or religious observances) or provide an explanation during office hours for consideration.
\item Attendance is worth \AttendanceWeight{} of the total course grade.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Grading of Attendance:}
\begin{itemize}
\item Exam 1 and Exam 2 are exempt from the attendance calculation. All other scheduled class meetings count unless the instructor exempts a meeting.
\item In Blackboard, an ``Excused'' status applies to an individual student and does not count as an absence. An exempt meeting is excluded from the attendance calculation for the entire class.
\item A ``Late'' status receives 50\% attendance credit for that meeting.
\item After the first three absences without penalty, each additional unexcused absence reduces the attendance grade proportionally across the remaining counted meetings.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Exams}
Three closed-book exams are scheduled: Exam 1: \ExamOneDate{}; Exam 2: \ExamTwoDate{}; Final Exam: \FinalExamStatus{}. Make up exams will be given at the discretion of the instructor.

\textbf{Exam Crib Sheets.} Crib sheet(s) are permitted in exams with the following specifications:
\begin{itemize}
\item Exam 1: one single-sided crib sheet. Exam 2: Exam 1 crib sheet plus a second single-sided crib sheet.
\item Each is single sided, hand-written (no tablet) by the student, and includes name and RIN.
\item Allowed: equations, brief notes, basic diagrams.
\item Disallowed: full solutions to problems and extensive sections of course notes.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Disability Support Services Exams.} Students with exam accommodations must follow the procedures below, consistent with RPI Testing Center policy:
\begin{itemize}
\item Students must provide the instructor with a current accommodation letter issued by RPI Access \& Neurodiverse Student Success as soon as possible.
\item Students are responsible for scheduling their own exams through the RPI Testing Center scheduling system. Time slots are limited and fill quickly, so students are encouraged to schedule as soon as possible based on the exam dates provided above/below. Book using the Testing Center request system.
\item For this course, DSS exams must be taken on the same calendar day as the scheduled in-class exam, with the exam starting between 8:00 AM and 12:00 PM, unless explicitly approved in writing by the instructor.
\item All exam requests require instructor approval through the Testing Center system. An exam appointment is not confirmed until it has been approved by both the instructor and the Testing Center.
\end{itemize}
Additional information regarding Testing Center policies and procedures is available at: \url{https://provost.rpi.edu/faculty-resources/testing-center}. Failure to submit appropriate documentation or to schedule an exam within the allowed time window may result in the exam being taken without accommodations.
