\section*{Institutional and Course Policies}
\textbf{Accessibility and inclusivity.} The instruction team strives to provide an equitable learning environment. Students with documented disabilities should contact the Office of Disability Services for Students and then contact the instructor to discuss implementation of accommodations.

\textbf{Technology.} Laptops and mobile phones are not permitted during class unless specifically authorized for a class activity or required by documented accommodation. Tablets are permitted for note-taking and course-related use.

\textbf{Academic integrity and generative AI.} Students are responsible for submitting their own work. Collaboration on homework and labs is encouraged, but submitted work must be the student's own. Generative AI and AI-assisted tools may be used to improve readability and language, but not to replace core learning tasks such as interpreting data, carrying out analysis, or drawing conclusions. Use must be disclosed when it contributes to submitted work.
