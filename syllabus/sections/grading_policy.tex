\textbf{Regrade Requests.} All grading re-evaluations (excluding exams) will be handled through Gradescope's regrade request system. Students must submit any regrade requests within one week of the grade being posted. After this one-week window, no regrade requests will be considered, regardless of the circumstances. It is the student's responsibility to review feedback promptly and submit requests within the stated timeframe. Exam re-evaluations must be made during office hours within two weeks of the return date.

\textbf{Final Grade Rounding.} Final course grades will be calculated according to the grading scale listed above. Grades will be rounded up to the nearest tenth of a percent (0.1\%). For example, a grade of 92.91\% will be recorded as 93.0\% (A). No additional considerations (e.g., participation, effort, or ``close calls'') will be applied beyond this rounding policy.

\textbf{Opportunities for Extra Credit.} After the two exams, students may submit a Root Cause Analysis (RCA) to analyze their exam performance. The RCA may redeem 5 points, weighted according to Option 1 grade distributions, on the original exam score. One point (1\%) of bonus credit is also available if final course evaluations are completed to 90\% or above for the whole class (all sections).

\textbf{Laptops and Mobile Phones.} To promote attention and reduce distractions, the use of laptops and mobile phones is not permitted during class, unless specifically authorized by the instructor for a class activity. Tablets are permitted for note-taking and course-related use. All other devices must be silenced and put away after attendance is taken. Students with documented accommodations may use devices as required. In case of emergency, notify the instructor and step outside if a phone is needed. Unauthorized use of laptops or phones may result in being marked absent.
