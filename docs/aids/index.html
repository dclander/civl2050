Interactive figures used in lecture. Each one opens in the browser and works on a phone or laptop. Nothing to install.

Class 01

Solids and fluids under shear

A solid shears to a fixed angle and holds it. A fluid shears at a rate that continues for as long as the stress acts. That difference is the definition of a fluid.

Class 03

Centre of pressure on a plane surface

Why the resultant hydrostatic force acts below the centroid, and what sets the distance. Shape, depth and angle can be varied, with and without atmospheric pressure.

Class 04

Curved surfaces and the fluid free body

A curved surface has no single plane on which pressure acts. Isolating the fluid behind it reduces the problem to a plane face and a weight.

Class 04

Pressure on the boundary of a submerged body

Pressure depends on depth and on the shape of the boundary, not on what the body is made of. Changing the contents changes only the weight.

Class 05

Flow kinematics

One velocity field throughout: the field itself, the acceleration of a particle moving through it, the unsteady case, and streamlines, pathlines, streaklines and timelines compared on the same flow.

Class 05 · 06

The Navier–Stokes equations

Gradient form and the expanded x component, with fluid statics and Bernoulli as special cases. Then the open question, the Burgers vortex in section and in three dimensions, and the status of the 2026 forced blow-up construction.
