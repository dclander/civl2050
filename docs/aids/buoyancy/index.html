Pressure on the boundary depends on depth, not on contents.

Pressure acts on the boundary. It depends on depth and on the fluid, and on nothing else. Changing the contents of the block leaves every pressure arrow unchanged. Only the weight changes, and the weight determines whether the block rises or sinks.
